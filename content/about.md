The Chaarging Kangaroo is here to promote the use of electric vehicles in Australia. We are a community of EV enthusiasts who want to see more people driving electric cars and using renewable energy.

The Charging Kangaroo Card is our way to help the EV community by providing a simple and convenient way to access charging stations across the country. Making it easier than ever to keep your EV charged and ready to go.
